What Guardian does

Everything you tend to, visible in one place.

Guardianships

Create a guardianship for anything in your care — a pet, a parent, a creative project, a document. Each one grows as you tend to it.

Care Log

Log moments with notes, photos, and video. Build a timeline of care that you can look back on — and share when it counts.

Pattern Insights

Guardian notices what you might miss. "Miso has been off food 3 of the last 7 days." Patterns surface before they become problems.

Shareable Care Pages

Send a link to a vet, babysitter, sibling, or specialist. No app needed. You control what they see, and for how long.

Care Garden

A living visual of all your guardianships. Each plant's health reflects how recently you've tended to it. Beautiful. And honest.

Medical Export

Export a clean, dated PDF timeline of any guardianship. Ready for vets, specialists, insurance, schools — whoever needs the full picture.

Access levels

You decide who sees what, and for how long.

Full Control
Owner

Creates and manages the guardianship. Controls all sharing permissions. Can export, archive, and transfer ownership.

Can Add + Log
Contributor

Can log entries, add photos and video, and annotate. Perfect for a partner, caregiver, or collaborator who's actively involved.

Read Only
Viewer

Can read the log and export a PDF. No editing. Ideal for a vet visit, a handoff to a sitter, or a specialist who needs context fast.
